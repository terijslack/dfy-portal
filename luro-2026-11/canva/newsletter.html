November Birthstones
Every gemstone has a story.
This November, we're getting to know topaz and citrine, two birthstones with plenty of color and a few surprises.
Whether you're celebrating a birthday or simply love learning about jewelry, we're glad you're here.
Topaz
Think topaz is always golden? It can also be blue, pink, violet, brown, and, rarely, red. Most blue topaz gets its color from irradiation and heat treatment. A little lore: Ancient Greeks believed topaz gave them strength.
Today, blue topaz also marks a fourth wedding anniversary.
 
Citrine
With shades from yellow to brownish orange, citrine is quartz with a sunny disposition. Its name is believed to come from the French citron, meaning lemon. Most citrine sold today is heat-treated amethyst.
Citrine is also the gem for a thirteenth wedding anniversary.
Keep the Glow
Warm, soapy water is a safe choice for both gems.
Skip steam cleaning for either, and avoid ultrasonic cleaners for topaz.
Topaz can chip, so a little care goes a long way.
